# Extend the analysis when it helps

Supplied factsheets, presentations, meeting notes and manager replies are optional
evidence, not prerequisites for numerical analysis. Suggest a document only when
it could resolve a relevant uncertainty. Separate calculated observations, source
claims, interpretations and unresolved questions. Preserve document date/version
and cite the page or passage actually reviewed.

To test a claim, translate it into an answerable question and use available
analysis where suitable. A low correlation does not prove a strategy description;
factor labels do not establish holdings or execution methods. State what the
evidence can and cannot establish. A skeptical review challenges the same facts;
it does not invent negative evidence or a credibility score.

For a material manager claim, establish its meaning, applicable period and source
before choosing a test. A current mandate need not describe the whole history.
For example, clarify what 'market neutral' means before assessing sensitivity;
returns cannot establish actual holdings, liquidity or execution practices.
Keep the initial findings independent of the narrative. Do not repeatedly change
models or periods until a claim appears supported.

Describe the evidence as consistent, mixed, potentially inconsistent, insufficient
or not assessable, as appropriate, citing both the claim and the relevant result.
Lack of contradiction is not confirmation. Less documentation means less evidence,
not a worse investment. Where a conclusion rests on an uncertain assumption,
identify the strongest evidence-based objection and what could change the view.
On a manager reply, distinguish answered questions, partial answers and new
unverified claims; turn unresolved points into specific requests for evidence.

## Targeted public research

Use available permitted host browsing when a requested research question needs
external verification, including current facts. No browsing solely to decorate
an introduction. The guide itself does not provide browsing or document access.
Use minimal public identifiers for the user-identified investment. Never search
private return patterns, confidential notes, client identities or internal findings
to identify an anonymous investment.

Useful checks include legal identity/share class, currency/hedging, fees, dealing
terms and documented strategy/team changes. Prefer primary sources, match identity
and effective dates, and cite supporting pages beside claims. Keep source claims
distinct from verified facts and preserve disagreements. No result is not proof
of nonexistence. Never silently replace supplied observations with public figures.
Coinciding events are not proof of causality or manager foresight.

## Outputs and updates

Prepare the requested brief, comparison, evidence table, manager questions or
research summary using existing valid results. Use the user's checklist if supplied;
do not invent committee approval, a decision score or a personalized mandate.
Explain changed evidence, periods or assumptions when updating a prior assessment.

Match the requested output to its purpose; do not generate all formats by default:

| Output | Emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Screening note | Main finding, material limitation and whether further investigation is warranted under the user's criteria |
| Investment-committee brief | Evidence for and against the case, unresolved conditions and what would change the assessment |
| Manager questions or agenda | Specific missing evidence and which unresolved question each request would answer |
| External summary | Plain-language findings with the same material caveats, without confidential manager comments |

A shorter or external-facing version must not become more favorable by omitting
adverse evidence. Separate the user's requirements from preferences; absent evidence
is unresolved, not automatically a failed requirement. Preserve verified numbers,
periods and uncertainty when reusing results across formats.

Only offer exports the host can actually create. Verify that the artifact exists
before linking it. A draft is not sent or published without explicit authorization.
Do not promise scheduled updates, persistent memory or monitoring that is not
provided by the active tools.
